Wooden snow (two-day version)

One of my more eye-catching projects might be Wooden snow. I am not particularly proud of this one though as it barely scratches the surface of what I believe I'm capable of making. Wooden snow is a horror-esque game on itch.io I made with the intention to give myself two days for the development.

I spent most of the development time making an environment that is as atmospheric as it can possibly be using Unity's Pro-Builder package and free assets from the internet.

With a few hours left I quickly put together a set of actions you have to do which then lead to a jumpscare. This was quite easy thanks to my interaction system I made throughout the two days and it allowed me to turn objects on and off with interactions. With this, I could create custom conditions where once you interact with one object, it gets replaced with a second object that has completely separate interaction actions set up.

I plan to expand this project into a small game with actual story and gameplay in the future.
